Capital One is a US money-center bank and financial services company offering credit cards, checking and savings accounts, loans, and auto financing. Through the Capital One DevExchange developer program the bank publishes a catalog of partner-facing production APIs for account lookup, transaction authorizations, credit offers, customer transactions, credit card applications, auto financing, proactive prescreen, partner account summary, shop with rewards, and public key sharing for client authentication. Access is secured with OAuth 2.0 and a public sandbox supports pre-production integration, with production access gated behind partner approval. Developer documentation is HTML-only (the portal is a single-page app); Capital One does not publish downloadable OpenAPI specifications. As an FDX member, Capital One also supports consumer-permissioned open-finance data access through aggregators in the US voluntary open-banking context.

**Areas:** Auto Finance · Authorization · Banking · Credit Cards · Credit Offers · DevExchange · Financial Services · FDX · Open Banking · Authentication · Payments · Rewards

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Capital One: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
